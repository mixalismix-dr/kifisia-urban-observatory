# Kifisia Urban Observatory

The observatory presents Kifisia's mapped urban environment to general visitors. Its current focus is visual exploration of available spatial data. A guided flyover and planning studies may follow.

## Language

**Public exploration**:
The visitor experience of navigating and inspecting Kifisia's mapped data as a three-dimensional city. Direct map layers take priority over a guided tour.

**Guided flyover**:
A short, visitor-controlled tour through selected Kifisia places, showing their mapped setting and why each place is included. Its route remains undecided until the underlying map is ready.

**Map layer**:
Spatial data displayed directly in the Kifisia map so visitors can see and inspect its features.

**Source link**:
A reference to an external map or dataset whose features are not displayed directly in the Kifisia map.

**Planning study**:
A later, question-led assessment of a proposed urban change using suitable spatial data and a stated method.

**Mapped object**:
A building, place, route, vehicle, or landscape feature displayed by the observatory with its available source information.

## Example dialogue

Developer: "The municipal works map is in our source list. Is it a map layer?"

Domain expert: "Yes. Its published work features appear directly in our map. The source link remains so visitors can inspect the municipality's original map. Complete the usable map layers before designing the guided flyover."
